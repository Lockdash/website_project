Our News

Let us tell you what's happened in the past several weeks...

Welcome to Our News. This is the central page for updates, announcements, and glimpses into the life of our bookstore community. We are always evolving, and this space is dedicated to sharing that journey with you.

Here, you'll find information about our upcoming events, such as the launch of our Summer Book Camp for young readers, where weekly themes transform reading into an adventure. As the seasons change, look forward to details on our annual Spooky Halloween Spectacular, featuring curated horror displays and eerie story readings.

We also shine a light on unique cultural experiences, like our upcoming collaborative concert, "An Evening with Beethoven," blending live classical music with literary biography. For our regular community, we post the monthly selections and meeting details for our Book Club, your invitation to deeper discussion and connection.

Beyond events, we share curated reading lists, highlight new arrivals, and offer behind-the-scenes notes on our seasonal shop transformations. Check back often to stay connected with our story. The next chapter is always being written, and we're glad you're here to read it with us.
