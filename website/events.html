Upcoming events

Summer book camp

Trade screen time for story time! Our Summer Book Camp for young readers ages 7-12 is here. Each week dives into a new adventure theme—fantasy worlds, space odysseys, historical quests—with daily reading sessions, themed crafts, and engaging book club discussions. Led by our enthusiastic educators, campers will create book journals, make new friends, and discover the magic of getting lost in a great book. Sign up for one week or the whole season of literary fun! Limited spots.

Literature club meeting

Find your next favorite read and your next best friend. Join our welcoming community at our monthly Book Club! We meet on the first Tuesday of each month to discuss a pre-selected title—a mix of contemporary hits and hidden gems. Enjoy lively conversation, themed snacks, and 15% off the next month's pick. No pressure, just passion for stories. All are welcome, from seasoned bibliophiles to those just starting their reading journey. Let's talk books!

Beethoven concert

Experience the genius of Beethoven as you’ve never before! In collaboration with the City Chamber Ensemble, we present an intimate concert within our store's walls. Enjoy masterpieces like Moonlight Sonata and Für Elise performed live, paired with readings from historical biographies about the composer's remarkable life. A perfect fusion of literature and music. Reserve your seat for this unique acoustic evening on November 15th. Tickets include a complimentary beverage.

Halloween party

Unearth the perfect spine-tingling tale this Halloween! Our store transforms into a haunted literary den with a 20% discount on all horror, mystery, and supernatural fiction. Explore our curated display of classic chillers and modern thrillers. Don't miss our special events: scary story readings for all ages on October 29th and a costume contest for the best literary character on the 31st! Come for the frights, stay for the delights.
